# **My Favorite Movie: John Wick** 
## 🎥About the Movie
John Wick is an American action-thriller film directed by Chad Stahelski and starring Keanu Reeves.
It tells the story of a legendary hitman who comes out of retirement to seek revenge after a tragic loss.
Known for its stylish action, deep emotion, and breathtaking choreography, John Wick redefined modern action movies.

“People keep asking if I’m back... Yeah, I’m thinkin’ I’m back.” – **John Wick**

**⭐ Main Characters**

**John Wick** – A former assassin known as Baba Yaga, feared by the entire underworld.

**Winston** – The calm and intelligent manager of The Continental Hotel.

**Charon** – The polite and loyal concierge who assists Wick at the Continental.

**Sofia** – A fierce ally who helps Wick in his mission with her deadly trained dogs.

**The Bowery King** – A powerful underground leader who provides Wick with information and weapons.

**Viggo Tarasov** – A crime lord who underestimates John’s true power.

# **My Favorite Scenes**

**Home Invasion** – The turning point that brings John back to his old life.

 **Red Circle Club Fight** – A stylish and intense battle filled with precision and chaos.

**The Continental Showdown** – Loyalty and betrayal collide in a rule-breaking fight.

 **Parabellum Finale** – John faces endless assassins in a breathtaking gun-fu sequence.

 # **Why I Love It**

Incredible fight choreography and realistic combat scenes.

A mysterious world with its own rules and hierarchy.

Keanu Reeves’ dedication and emotion in every performance.

A story about strength, loss, and unstoppable willpower.
